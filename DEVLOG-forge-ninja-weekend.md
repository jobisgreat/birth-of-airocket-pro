### What This Rocket Does

* A **self-improving Lead Rocket** — it finds best-fit local businesses, qualifies them, and stages a real offer for a real owner.
* A live **Mission Control** dashboard — pipeline, review console, revenue math, outbound tracking, and sending performance on one screen, real data, refreshed on demand.
* A **closed feedback loop** — it records everything, analyzes its own results weekly, and proposes its own improvements inside pre-agreed guardrails. It never edits its own code; it proposes, the founder disposes.
* A **compliance-first spine** — every high-stakes action waits for a human gate. The gate is a feature, not a delay.

### The Discipline That Made It Fast

* **Honest accounting.** "Built" ≠ "shipped" until the customer journey is tested end-to-end. Every line of this log is a thing that actually ran.
* **Dry-run everything.** A safe simulation caught a double-send bug and a bad-data bounce *before* they could ever touch a real prospect. You never skip the dry run.
* **Secrets never leave the vault.** Keys stay out of the repo, full stop.
